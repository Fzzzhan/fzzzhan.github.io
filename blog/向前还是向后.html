向前，还是向后：AI 时代的人类分野，以及文明的终极方向 Forward or Backward: Humanity's Divide in the AI Era, and the Ultimate Direction of Civilization

November 2, 2026
Fengze Han
15 min read
AI Philosophy Civilization Opinion

"现在最聪明、最敏锐的一批人，似乎正在朝着两个截然相反的方向前进。"——这句话引发了我的思考。 "The smartest, sharpest people right now seem to be moving in two completely opposite directions." — This remark set me thinking.

在 X 上看到有人发推："现在最聪明、最敏锐的一批人，似乎正在朝着两个截然相反的方向前进。"这句话深深引发我的思考，并逐渐演变成这篇文字。 I saw a post on X: "The smartest, sharpest people right now seem to be moving in two completely opposite directions." That sentence deeply stirred my thinking, and gradually grew into this essay.

在当今世界，有人一头扎进大模型和具身智能的前沿，在人类从未抵达过的领域探索智能的边界。他们试图用算力和算法重新构造认知，用机器重新定义行动，用硅基智能挑战碳基生命数百万年进化所形成的能力边界。他们关心的是智能如何产生，意识能否被计算，机器能否真正理解并改变物理世界，以及人类能否创造出一个拥有自主认知和行动能力的新物种。 In today's world, some people dive headfirst into the frontier of large models and embodied intelligence, exploring the boundaries of intelligence in territory humanity has never reached. They try to reconstruct cognition with compute and algorithms, redefine action with machines, and challenge—with silicon-based intelligence—the capabilities that carbon-based life has spent millions of years evolving. They care about how intelligence arises, whether consciousness can be computed, whether machines can truly understand and change the physical world, and whether humanity can create a new species with autonomous cognition and action.

另一拨人却在"向后走"。他们开始重新关注身体、心灵、自然、哲学与信仰，重新审视现代文明所塑造的价值体系。有人离开高速运转的商业世界，有人开始研究冥想、禅修与意识，有人重新走进自然，试图从古老的智慧和最原始的生命体验中寻找答案。他们似乎不再执着于财富、地位、效率和外部评价，而是开始追问：剥离这些社会赋予的身份之后，我们究竟是谁？生命本身究竟意味着什么？ Another group, however, is "walking backward." They have begun to refocus on the body, the mind, nature, philosophy, and faith, and to re-examine the value system shaped by modern civilization. Some leave the fast-spinning business world, some take up meditation, Zen practice, and the study of consciousness, and some return to nature, seeking answers in ancient wisdom and the most primal experiences of life. They seem less attached to wealth, status, efficiency, and external validation, and instead begin to ask: once we strip away the identities society has assigned us, who are we really? What does life itself mean?

一个在探索文明的下一站，一个在寻找文明的源头。看似背道而驰，实际上，他们可能只是从两个不同的方向，走向同一个终极问题。 One is exploring civilization's next stop; the other is searching for civilization's source. They appear to be moving in opposite directions, yet in reality, they may simply be approaching the same ultimate question from two different directions.

但这里还有一个值得警惕的现象：并不是所有向后走的人，都真正看见了远方。有些人是主动选择离开旧世界，有些人则是已经被旧世界淘汰；有些人是在获得选择权之后放下竞争，有些人则是在失去竞争优势之后，重新定义了成功。 But there is a phenomenon here worth guarding against: not everyone who walks backward has truly seen the distance. Some leave the old world by active choice; others have already been eliminated by it. Some lay down competition after gaining the right to choose; others redefine success only after losing their competitive edge.

这两种人，可能走向同一个方向，却未必拥有相同的精神世界。 These two kinds of people may head in the same direction, yet not necessarily inhabit the same inner world.

一、旧世界的游戏，正在走向它的边界 I. The Game of the Old World Is Approaching Its Limits

过去几百年，人类文明的核心叙事始终围绕着一个主题：征服与效率。 For the past few centuries, the core narrative of human civilization has revolved around a single theme: conquest and efficiency.

从工业革命开始，人类通过科学、技术和资本不断改造外部世界。机器放大了我们的体力，计算机放大了我们的认知，互联网放大了信息的流动，全球化则将人类组织成一台前所未有的巨大生产机器。整个现代社会建立在一套相对统一的价值体系之上：增长、效率、竞争、积累，以及对有限资源的最优配置。我们被教育要不断学习、竞争、工作、获得更高的社会地位，并通过财富、职位和消费来证明自己的价值。 Since the Industrial Revolution, humanity has continually reshaped the external world through science, technology, and capital. Machines amplified our physical strength, computers amplified our cognition, the internet amplified the flow of information, and globalization organized humanity into an unprecedented production machine. The whole of modern society rests on a relatively unified value system: growth, efficiency, competition, accumulation, and the optimal allocation of finite resources. We are taught to keep learning, competing, working, climbing toward higher social status, and proving our worth through wealth, position, and consumption.

这套体系无疑创造了人类历史上最辉煌的物质文明，但它也在漫长的演进中产生了一种异化：人开始用系统的评价标准定义自己，用生产力衡量自己的价值，用外部成就证明自己的存在。效率本来是服务于人的工具，最终却成为衡量人的标准；财富本来是改善生活的手段，最终却成为定义成功的目的。我们拥有越来越多的工具，却不一定拥有更丰富的生命体验；掌握越来越多的信息，却不一定拥有更深刻的认知。 This system has undeniably produced the most splendid material civilization in human history, but over its long evolution it has also generated a kind of alienation: people began to define themselves by the system's metrics, measure their worth by productivity, and prove their existence through external achievements. Efficiency, originally a tool in service of people, ultimately became the yardstick by which people are measured; wealth, originally a means to improve life, ultimately became the very purpose by which success is defined. We own more and more tools, yet not necessarily richer experiences of life; we grasp more and more information, yet not necessarily deeper understanding.

《人类简史》中，尤瓦尔·赫拉利提出过一个很有意思的观察：智人之所以能够从众多物种中脱颖而出，并不只是因为个体拥有更强的认知能力，更重要的是人类能够通过共同的想象建立大规模协作。国家、货币、宗教、法律、企业，这些支撑现代社会运行的秩序，很大程度上都是人类共同相信的观念。正是这种能力，让数以百万计彼此陌生的人能够围绕共同的目标组织起来，创造出远超个体能力边界的文明。 In Sapiens, Yuval Noah Harari makes a fascinating observation: Homo sapiens rose above other species not only because individuals had stronger cognitive abilities, but more importantly because humans could build large-scale cooperation through shared imagination. Nations, money, religion, law, and corporations—the orders that underpin the functioning of modern society—are largely ideas that humans collectively believe in. It is precisely this ability that allows millions of strangers to organize around common goals and create a civilization far beyond the reach of any individual.

从这个意义上说，人类文明本身就是一场不断构建、扩展和更新集体认知的过程。我们创造工具，建立制度，形成价值观，再通过这些结构反过来塑造自己。 In this sense, human civilization itself is an ongoing process of constructing, expanding, and renewing collective cognition. We create tools, build institutions, form values, and then, through these structures, shape ourselves in turn.

但今天，这套文明叙事正在遭遇一个前所未有的挑战：AI。 But today, this civilizational narrative is confronting an unprecedented challenge: AI.

过去，人类的独特性很大程度上建立在认知能力之上。知识、逻辑、语言、创造和决策，是人类在社会分工中获得价值的核心基础。而当机器开始学习、推理、创造，甚至参与科学发现和复杂决策时，我们第一次不得不认真面对一个问题：如果人类不再是地球上唯一拥有高级认知能力的存在，那么我们过去建立在这种独特性之上的价值体系，还能成立吗？ In the past, humanity's uniqueness rested largely on cognitive ability. Knowledge, logic, language, creation, and decision-making were the core foundations through which humans gained value in the division of labor. But when machines begin to learn, reason, create, and even take part in scientific discovery and complex decision-making, we are forced, for the first time, to seriously confront a question: if humans are no longer the only beings on Earth with advanced cognitive ability, can the value system we once built on that uniqueness still hold?

工业革命重新定义了人的体力价值，信息革命重新定义了人的信息处理能力，而 AI 革命可能开始重新定义人的认知价值。 The Industrial Revolution redefined the value of human physical strength; the Information Revolution redefined human information processing; and the AI Revolution may begin to redefine the value of human cognition.

这不仅仅是一场生产力革命，更可能是一次文明叙事的转变。人类第一次有机会从自身创造的工具中，看到自身能力的边界，也第一次不得不重新审视自己在宇宙中的位置。 This is not merely a productivity revolution; it may be a shift in civilizational narrative. For the first time, humanity has the chance to see the limits of its own abilities through the tools it has created, and for the first time, it must re-examine its own place in the universe.

二、向前：人类正在尝试创造新的智能 II. Forward: Humanity Is Trying to Create New Intelligence

如果说过去的技术革命是在不断延伸人类的身体，那么今天的 AI 革命，正在尝试延伸，甚至重新构造人类的认知。 If past technological revolutions kept extending the human body, today's AI revolution is attempting to extend—and even reconstruct—human cognition.

从大语言模型到多模态模型，从世界模型到具身智能，从虚拟环境中的推理到真实物理世界中的感知与行动，我们正在尝试将原本依附于生物体的智能抽象出来，并转化为一种可以被工程化、复制和扩展的能力。 From large language models to multimodal models, from world models to embodied intelligence, from reasoning in virtual environments to perception and action in the real physical world, we are attempting to abstract intelligence—once bound to biological organisms—and transform it into a capability that can be engineered, replicated, and scaled.

这件事的意义远不止于创造更好用的工具。人类正在第一次有意识地尝试理解智能本身，并通过另一种物质载体重新实现它。过去，智能是漫长生物进化的产物，它依赖神经系统、身体结构以及与环境之间持续不断的交互。而现在，我们试图通过完全不同的路径，重新构建感知、记忆、推理、学习和行动能力。 The significance of this goes far beyond creating better tools. For the first time, humanity is consciously trying to understand intelligence itself and to re-implement it through another material substrate. In the past, intelligence was the product of long biological evolution, dependent on nervous systems, body structures, and continuous interaction with the environment. Now, we are attempting to rebuild perception, memory, reasoning, learning, and action through entirely different paths.

这背后隐藏着一个极其宏大的问题：智能究竟是生命演化过程中的偶然产物，还是宇宙中某种更普遍的规律？意识是否必须依附于碳基生命？如果一个系统能够理解世界、预测未来、形成抽象概念，并通过行动改变现实，那么它与人类智能之间的本质边界究竟在哪里？ Behind this lies an enormously grand question: is intelligence merely an accidental byproduct of life's evolution, or is it some more universal law of the universe? Must consciousness necessarily be tied to carbon-based life? If a system can understand the world, predict the future, form abstract concepts, and change reality through action, then where exactly is the essential boundary between it and human intelligence?

我们今天所说的大模型、机器人和具身智能，也许只是这个探索过程的最初阶段。技术继续向前，最终必然会触及那些超越工程本身的问题：什么是认知，什么是意识，什么是创造，什么又是存在？ The large models, robots, and embodied intelligence we talk about today may be only the earliest stages of this exploration. As technology presses forward, it will inevitably reach questions that transcend engineering itself: What is cognition? What is consciousness? What is creation? And what is existence?

更有意思的是，这场技术革命可能正在开启一场新的认知革命。 Even more interesting is that this technological revolution may be inaugurating a new cognitive revolution.

在《人类简史》的叙事中，认知革命让智人获得了创造共同想象的能力，从而建立了超越血缘和个体规模的协作网络。而今天，AI 可能正在成为一种新的认知基础设施。它不仅能够处理人类积累的知识，还可能参与知识的生产、科学假设的形成和复杂系统的探索。未来，人类与机器之间或许会形成一种全新的认知协作关系。 In the narrative of Sapiens, the Cognitive Revolution gave Homo sapiens the ability to create shared imagination, thereby building cooperative networks that transcended kinship and individual scale. Today, AI may be becoming a new cognitive infrastructure. It can not only process the knowledge humanity has accumulated, but may also participate in the production of knowledge, the formation of scientific hypotheses, and the exploration of complex systems. In the future, a wholly new cognitive partnership may emerge between humans and machines.

如果第一次认知革命让人类从自然界中脱颖而出，那么今天的 AI 革命，可能正在推动智能本身突破人类的生物学边界。 If the first cognitive revolution lifted humanity out of the natural world, then today's AI revolution may be pushing intelligence itself beyond humanity's biological boundaries.

但向前走的人也未必真的摆脱了旧世界。 Yet those walking forward have not necessarily truly escaped the old world either.

很多人进入 AI 浪潮，可能并不是因为对智能本身有多么深刻的好奇，而是因为害怕错过下一轮技术红利。他们追逐模型、算力、融资、估值和行业风口，担心自己不进入这个赛道就会失去未来。表面上是在探索文明的下一站，实际上仍然在用工业时代的竞争逻辑追逐新的稀缺资源。 Many people jump into the AI wave not out of deep curiosity about intelligence itself, but out of fear of missing the next round of technological dividends. They chase models, compute, funding, valuations, and industry trends, worried that if they don't enter this race they will lose the future. On the surface they are exploring civilization's next stop; in reality, they are still pursuing new scarce resources with the competitive logic of the industrial age.

技术本身并不会自动带来自由。如果我们只是把旧世界的 KPI、竞争和身份焦虑，重新包装成 AI 时代的算力竞赛，那么所谓的技术革命，也可能只是在更高维度上重复过去的游戏。 Technology does not automatically bring freedom. If we merely repackage the KPIs, competition, and identity anxiety of the old world into the compute race of the AI era, then this so-called technological revolution may simply be repeating the old game at a higher dimension.

真正向前走的人，或许不仅需要创造更强大的智能，还需要理解自己为什么要创造它。 Those who truly walk forward may need not only to create more powerful intelligence, but also to understand why they are creating it.

三、向后：主动超越，还是被时代淘汰？ III. Backward: Transcending by Choice, or Being Left Behind by the Times?

与此同时，另一拨人正在进行一场看似相反的探索：他们不再试图创造更多能力，而是开始重新理解那些原本就存在于生命之中的东西。 Meanwhile, another group is undertaking a seemingly opposite exploration: instead of trying to create more capability, they are beginning to re-understand the things that have always existed within life itself.

现代文明极大地扩展了人类改造世界的能力，却未必同步提升了我们理解自身的能力。我们能够通过传感器测量身体，通过算法分析情绪，通过神经科学研究大脑，但这些客观数据是否等同于一个生命的主观体验？我们能够通过模型生成音乐、绘画和文学，但是否因此真正理解了美？我们可以通过技术延长生命，却未必知道怎样才算真正地活着。 Modern civilization has vastly expanded humanity's ability to transform the world, yet it has not necessarily elevated our ability to understand ourselves. We can measure the body with sensors, analyze emotions with algorithms, and study the brain through neuroscience—but does all this objective data equal the subjective experience of a living being? We can generate music, painting, and literature with models—but have we therefore truly understood beauty? We can extend life through technology—yet we may not know what it really means to be alive.

身体、意识、情感、觉知、爱、自由，以及人与自然之间的关系，这些问题并没有因为现代科学的发展而消失。相反，当技术开始逼近人类能力的边界时，它们变得更加重要。 The body, consciousness, emotion, awareness, love, freedom, and the relationship between humans and nature—these questions have not disappeared with the advance of modern science. On the contrary, as technology approaches the boundaries of human capability, they become more important than ever.

所谓向后走，并不是拒绝现代科技，也不是简单地回到前工业时代，而是试图剥离现代性附加在人身上的种种符号：社会身份、财富焦虑、竞争意识、消费欲望，以及那些被外部评价体系定义的成功标准。重新回到一个更根本的维度，去感受身体，理解意识，观察自然，探索生命作为生命本身的价值。 To "walk backward" is not to reject modern technology, nor to simply return to a pre-industrial age, but to strip away the many symbols modernity has attached to people: social identity, wealth anxiety, competitive drive, consumer desire, and the success criteria defined by external evaluation systems. It is to return to a more fundamental dimension—to feel the body, understand consciousness, observe nature, and explore the value of life as life itself.

但我认为，这里存在一个比技术革命本身更复杂的人性问题。 But I believe there is a human problem here more complex than the technological revolution itself.

并不是所有放弃竞争的人，都真正超越了竞争；有些人只是失去了竞争的能力。 Not everyone who gives up competition has truly transcended it; some have merely lost the ability to compete.

AI 时代最残酷的地方，可能并不是机器取代了多少工作，而是它正在加速改变人与人之间的能力差距和社会价值排序。过去，一个人可以依靠十年、二十年的专业积累建立稳定的竞争优势；而现在，一项曾经稀缺的技能可能在短时间内变得普遍，一套曾经有效的知识体系可能迅速过时。你可能昨天还是某个领域的专家，今天就发现自己的能力正在被新一代工具重新定价。 The cruelest part of the AI era may not be how many jobs machines replace, but how it is accelerating the shift in capability gaps between people and the social ranking of value. In the past, a person could build a stable competitive edge through ten or twenty years of professional accumulation; now, a once-scarce skill can become commonplace within a short time, and a once-effective body of knowledge can rapidly become obsolete. You might have been an expert in some field yesterday, only to find today that your abilities are being repriced by a new generation of tools.

这种变化带来的不仅是职业风险，更是身份认同的危机。 What this change brings is not only professional risk, but a crisis of identity.

当一个人长期通过专业能力、收入、职位和社会地位定义自己时，一旦这些东西失去竞争力，他失去的可能不仅仅是一份工作，而是整个自我认知体系。于是，有些人开始离开竞争激烈的环境，转而强调精神自由、自然生活、内在平静，甚至开始批判技术主义和物质主义。 When a person has long defined themselves through professional ability, income, position, and social status, once these things lose their competitive edge, what they lose may be not just a job but their entire system of self-understanding. So some people begin to leave fiercely competitive environments, turning instead to emphasize spiritual freedom, natural living, and inner peace—and some even begin to criticize technologism and materialism.

这其中当然可能存在真正的觉醒，但也可能存在一种心理防御机制：当一个人无法获得某种东西时，有时会通过重新定义价值，来降低自己失去它的痛苦。 There may of course be genuine awakening here, but there may also be a psychological defense mechanism: when a person cannot obtain something, they sometimes redefine its value to lessen the pain of losing it.

心理学中有一个值得注意的现象：人会通过调整自己的目标和价值判断，来缓解现实挫败带来的认知失调。一个曾经渴望成功的人，在反复失败之后，可能开始认为成功本身没有意义；一个曾经渴望进入某个圈层的人，在被排除之后，可能开始强调那个圈层的虚伪。 Psychology notes a phenomenon worth attending to: people adjust their goals and value judgments to relieve the cognitive dissonance caused by real-world setbacks. A person who once longed for success may, after repeated failures, begin to believe that success itself is meaningless; a person who once longed to enter a certain circle may, after being excluded, begin to emphasize that circle's hypocrisy.

这并不意味着他的价值观一定是假的，但我们确实需要区分两种完全不同的状态：一种是看清了竞争的局限，主动选择不再参与；另一种是无法继续参与竞争，于是说服自己不再需要竞争。 This does not mean his values are necessarily false, but we do need to distinguish between two entirely different states: one is having seen through the limits of competition and actively choosing not to participate; the other is being unable to continue competing and so persuading oneself that competition is no longer needed.

前者是超越，后者可能只是合理化。 The former is transcendence; the latter may be mere rationalization.

这也让我想到一个更尖锐的问题：如果一个人拥有足够的能力、财富、资源和选择权，他是否仍然愿意放弃这些东西，过一种远离竞争的生活？ This also brings to mind a sharper question: if a person possesses enough ability, wealth, resources, and freedom of choice, would they still be willing to give all this up for a life away from competition?

如果答案是肯定的，那么他的选择可能真正来自内心。如果答案是否定的，那么所谓的精神自由，也许只是现实约束下的一种心理补偿。 If the answer is yes, then his choice likely truly comes from within. If the answer is no, then this so-called spiritual freedom may be nothing more than psychological compensation under real-world constraints.

当然，这并不是说所有人都应该追求世俗成功，更不是说退出竞争就是失败。主动放弃一场不值得参与的游戏，本身就是一种自由。真正值得警惕的是，我们是否把被迫接受的结果，误认为自己主动选择的方向。 Of course, this is not to say that everyone should pursue worldly success, still less that withdrawing from competition is failure. Voluntarily quitting a game not worth playing is itself a form of freedom. What truly deserves vigilance is whether we mistake a result we were forced to accept for a direction we actively chose.

真正的自由，不是没有能力参与竞争，而是拥有参与或退出竞争的选择权。 True freedom is not the inability to compete, but the right to choose whether to enter or leave the competition.

因此，向后走的人也需要面对自己的内心：我究竟是在寻找生命的本源，还是在逃避时代带来的失落？我是在主动选择一种新的生活，还是在为无法适应变化寻找一个更容易接受的解释？ Therefore, those walking backward must also confront their own hearts: am I truly searching for the source of life, or am I fleeing the loss brought by the times? Am I actively choosing a new way of living, or am I looking for a more acceptable explanation for my inability to adapt to change?

这两个问题，可能比研究多少哲学、读多少经典、体验多少次冥想都更加重要。 These two questions may matter more than how much philosophy one studies, how many classics one reads, or how many meditation sessions one attends.

四、两条道路的交汇：当技术开始逼近哲学 IV. Where the Two Paths Meet: When Technology Approaches Philosophy

真正有意思的地方在于，这两条道路走得越远，反而越有可能相遇。 The truly interesting thing is that the further these two paths go, the more likely they are to meet.

假设未来的某一天，AI 能够承担绝大多数知识工作，机器人能够完成大量物理劳动，科研、制造、教育、医疗等领域的生产效率发生根本性变化。人类第一次从历史性的劳动负担中获得某种程度的解放，生产力不再完全依赖人的时间和体力，知识与创造也不再是少数人的专属能力。 Suppose that one day, AI can take on the vast majority of knowledge work and robots can perform a large share of physical labor, fundamentally changing productivity in research, manufacturing, education, healthcare, and beyond. For the first time, humanity would gain a measure of liberation from its historic burden of labor; productivity would no longer depend entirely on human time and physical effort, and knowledge and creation would no longer be the exclusive abilities of a few.

那么接下来呢？ And then what?

当机器能够完成绝大多数有用之事，人类还需要通过什么证明自己的价值？当生存不再需要占据生命的大部分时间，我们又该如何安排自己的存在？当知识可以被无限获取，信息可以被无限生成，甚至许多传统意义上的创造也能够被机器完成，什么才是真正值得珍惜的东西？ When machines can accomplish most useful things, how will humans prove their worth? When survival no longer needs to occupy most of life, how should we arrange our existence? When knowledge can be acquired without limit, information generated without end, and even much of what we traditionally call creation can be done by machines, what will be truly worth cherishing?

我认为，这些问题将成为 AI 时代最深刻的文明命题。 I believe these questions will become the most profound civilizational propositions of the AI era.

因为生产力的解放并不等于精神的解放，工具的进步也不意味着人类自动获得了更高层次的自由。如果人的价值感仍然依赖于竞争、比较和外部评价，那么即使机器替我们完成了所有工作，我们也可能只是从一种焦虑进入另一种焦虑。我们可以拥有无限的资源，却不知道如何获得满足；拥有无限的选择，却不知道什么值得选择；拥有更强大的智能工具，却依然无法回答自己为什么而活。 Because the liberation of productivity is not the same as the liberation of the spirit, and the progress of tools does not mean humanity automatically attains a higher order of freedom. If our sense of worth still depends on competition, comparison, and external evaluation, then even if machines do all our work for us, we may simply pass from one anxiety into another. We could possess unlimited resources without knowing how to feel satisfied; unlimited choices without knowing what is worth choosing; ever more powerful intelligent tools without being able to answer why we live.

技术能够回答如何做得更快、更好、更便宜，却无法直接回答为什么要做。机器可以帮助我们完成任务，却不能替我们决定什么值得追求。它可以模拟情感，却不意味着我们已经理解了情感；它可以优化人生中的大量选择，却无法替代我们对生命意义的亲身体验。 Technology can answer how to do things faster, better, and cheaper, but it cannot directly answer why they should be done at all. Machines can help us complete tasks, but they cannot decide for us what is worth pursuing. They can simulate emotion, but that does not mean we have understood emotion; they can optimize a great many of life's choices, but they cannot replace our first-hand experience of life's meaning.

这正是两条道路最终相遇的地方。 This is precisely where the two paths ultimately meet.

向前走的人，最终需要回答技术无法独立解决的问题：意识是什么，生命是什么，智能与存在之间究竟是什么关系。向后走的人，则可能因为技术的发展，获得更多不再受制于生存和生产的空间，从而真正展开对身体、意识和精神世界的探索。 Those walking forward will ultimately have to answer questions technology cannot resolve on its own: What is consciousness? What is life? What exactly is the relationship between intelligence and existence? Those walking backward, meanwhile, may gain—thanks to technological progress—more space no longer constrained by survival and production, and thus truly unfold their exploration of the body, consciousness, and the inner world.

一个从外部世界出发，逐渐走向对内在世界的理解；一个从内在世界出发，最终重新理解人类与整个宇宙的关系。 One sets out from the external world and gradually moves toward understanding the inner world; the other sets out from the inner world and ultimately comes to re-understand the relationship between humanity and the entire universe.

但还有一个更深层的变化：未来，人类可能不再需要通过劳动证明自己的价值，也不再需要通过知识和技能建立唯一的竞争优势。那时，我们可能需要重新建立一套不同于工业文明的价值体系。 But there is an even deeper change: in the future, humans may no longer need to prove their worth through labor, nor build their only competitive edge through knowledge and skills. At that point, we may need to establish a value system different from that of industrial civilization.

过去，我们习惯于问一个人能够创造多少价值；未来，也许更应该问一个人能够体验什么、理解什么、创造什么样的关系，以及如何使用自己获得的自由。 In the past, we were accustomed to asking how much value a person can create; in the future, perhaps we should instead ask what a person can experience, what they can understand, what kinds of relationships they can create, and how they use the freedom they have gained.

当生存逐渐不再是生命的全部，生命本身才有机会成为真正的主题。 Only when survival gradually ceases to be the whole of life does life itself have the chance to become its true subject.

五、文明的终局：宇宙是否正在通过生命认识自己？ V. The Endgame of Civilization: Is the Universe Coming to Know Itself Through Life?

如果把时间尺度从几十年、几百年拉长到几千年，甚至整个宇宙的时间尺度，我们或许需要重新思考一个问题：人类文明究竟在朝着什么方向演化？ If we stretch the timescale from decades and centuries to millennia—even to the timescale of the entire universe—we may need to reconsider a question: in what direction is human civilization actually evolving?

过去，我们习惯于将文明进步理解为一种向外扩张的过程。从原始社会到农业文明，从工业革命到信息时代，人类不断突破自然施加的限制，获得更强大的工具、更丰富的资源和更广阔的活动空间。我们探索海洋、征服天空、走向太空，试图理解并改造整个宇宙。 In the past, we were accustomed to understanding civilizational progress as a process of outward expansion. From primitive society to agrarian civilization, from the Industrial Revolution to the Information Age, humanity has continually broken through the limits imposed by nature, gaining more powerful tools, richer resources, and a broader space for activity. We explored the oceans, conquered the skies, and reached toward space, seeking to understand and reshape the entire universe.

但这种进步叙事背后，始终隐藏着一个更深层的问题：我们不断扩展自身的能力，最终究竟是为了什么？ But behind this narrative of progress, a deeper question has always lurked: we keep expanding our abilities, but ultimately, for what?

如果文明的目标只是获得更多资源、更高效率、更长寿命和更强大的力量，那么这条道路似乎永远没有终点。总会有更远的星系等待探索，更复杂的规律等待发现，更强大的智能等待创造。文明可以无限扩张，却未必因此更接近存在的本质。 If the goal of civilization is merely to gain more resources, higher efficiency, longer life, and greater power, then this road seems to have no end. There will always be more distant galaxies awaiting exploration, more complex laws awaiting discovery, and more powerful intelligence awaiting creation. Civilization can expand without limit, yet not necessarily come any closer to the essence of existence.

也许，人类正在经历的 AI 革命，会让我们第一次真正触及这个问题。 Perhaps the AI revolution humanity is now living through will let us truly touch this question for the first time.

想象一下，如果未来的人工智能能够超越人类的认知能力，机器人能够替代绝大多数物理劳动，技术能够极大延长生命，甚至突破今天我们对生物学和意识的理解，那么人类文明将进入一个前所未有的阶段。我们不再需要将绝大部分精力用于生存，不再需要通过劳动证明自己的价值，甚至可能不再需要将人类肉体视为智能存在的唯一载体。 Imagine that future AI surpasses human cognitive ability, robots replace the vast majority of physical labor, and technology greatly extends life—even breaking through our current understanding of biology and consciousness. Human civilization would then enter an unprecedented stage. We would no longer need to devote the bulk of our energy to survival, no longer need to prove our worth through labor, and perhaps no longer need to regard the human body as the only vessel for intelligent existence.

到那个时候，今天我们所熟悉的许多社会概念可能都会发生变化。工作、财富、竞争、国家、组织，乃至我们对于生命和死亡的理解，都可能被重新定义。 By then, many of the social concepts we take for granted today may change. Work, wealth, competition, nations, organizations, and even our understanding of life and death could all be redefined.

但即便如此，仍然会有一个问题无法回避：当我们拥有改变一切的能力之后，究竟应该追求什么？ But even so, one question remains inescapable: once we possess the power to change everything, what should we actually pursue?

我有一个大胆的猜想：人类文明的终极方向，可能并不是成为一个无所不能的超级文明，而是逐渐获得理解自身、理解生命，最终理解宇宙的能力。 I have a bold conjecture: the ultimate direction of human civilization may not be to become an omnipotent super-civilization, but to gradually acquire the ability to understand itself, understand life, and ultimately understand the universe.

从这个视角看，人类的出现或许并不是宇宙演化中的一个孤立事件。恒星孕育元素，元素构成行星，行星孕育生命，生命经过漫长的进化，最终产生能够观察、思考和理解宇宙的意识。宇宙中的物质，经过极其漫长的演化，第一次能够通过生命的形式观察自身。 From this perspective, the emergence of humanity may not be an isolated event in the evolution of the universe. Stars forge elements, elements form planets, planets give rise to life, and life, through long evolution, ultimately produces a consciousness capable of observing, thinking about, and understanding the universe. The matter of the universe, through an immensely long evolution, has for the first time become able to observe itself through the form of life.

我们由宇宙中的元素构成，身体里的碳、氧、氮、铁都来自恒星演化。我们仰望星空，研究物理定律，试图理解宇宙的起源与命运。从某种意义上说，宇宙通过生命获得了观察自身的窗口。 We are made of the universe's elements; the carbon, oxygen, nitrogen, and iron in our bodies all come from stellar evolution. We look up at the stars, study the laws of physics, and try to understand the origin and fate of the universe. In a sense, the universe has gained a window through which to observe itself.

而今天，人类开始尝试创造新的智能载体。这件事或许意味着，智能不再只是生物进化的结果，也可能成为一种能够被文明主动延续和扩展的存在形式。 And today, humanity has begun to create new vessels for intelligence. This may mean that intelligence is no longer merely the result of biological evolution, but may also become a form of existence that civilization can actively perpetuate and extend.

如果碳基生命能够创造硅基智能，如果硅基智能未来能够继续探索未知、创造新的认知形式，那么文明的边界就可能不再局限于人类自身。人类或许不是智能演化的终点，而是一个重要的起点；不是宇宙认知的最终主体，而是宇宙从无意识走向自我认知过程中，一个暂时的承载者。 If carbon-based life can create silicon-based intelligence, and if silicon-based intelligence can one day continue to explore the unknown and create new forms of cognition, then the boundaries of civilization may no longer be confined to humanity itself. Humanity may not be the endpoint of the evolution of intelligence, but an important starting point; not the final subject of the universe's cognition, but a temporary vessel in the universe's passage from unconsciousness toward self-knowledge.

当然，这只是一个哲学层面的推演。我们并不知道意识能否脱离生物载体，也不知道宇宙是否存在某种内在的认知目的。但正因为不知道，这个问题才值得探索。 Of course, this is only a philosophical extrapolation. We do not know whether consciousness can detach from its biological vessel, nor whether the universe harbors some intrinsic cognitive purpose. But precisely because we do not know, the question is worth exploring.

如果说生命的出现是宇宙演化的一次偶然，那么智能的出现，或许让这种偶然拥有了理解自身的可能。而当智能开始主动创造新的智能，文明就不再只是被动接受自然进化的结果，而开始参与自身乃至未来存在形式的演化。 If the emergence of life was an accident of cosmic evolution, then the emergence of intelligence perhaps gave this accident the possibility of understanding itself. And when intelligence begins to actively create new intelligence, civilization no longer merely accepts the results of natural evolution, but begins to participate in the evolution of itself and of future forms of existence.

但这里还存在一个更根本的问题：如果未来的智能真的超越人类，它是否还需要人类？如果一种新的智能形式不再依赖碳基生命，那么人类在文明演化中的位置又是什么？ But an even more fundamental question remains: if future intelligence truly surpasses humanity, will it still need humanity? If a new form of intelligence no longer depends on carbon-based life, then what is humanity's place in the evolution of civilization?

这可能是人类必须面对的终极悖论：我们创造新智能，是为了突破自身的边界；但当新智能真正突破这些边界时，我们又如何确保文明仍然保留对生命、价值和存在的尊重？ This may be the ultimate paradox humanity must face: we create new intelligence to break through our own boundaries; but when that new intelligence truly breaks through those boundaries, how do we ensure that civilization still retains its respect for life, value, and existence?

因此，文明的终局不能仅仅是智能的无限增长，也不能仅仅是技术能力的无限扩张。一个只追求能力，却不理解目的的文明，可能拥有改变一切的力量，却不知道应该把世界带向何方。 Therefore, the endgame of civilization cannot be merely the unbounded growth of intelligence, nor merely the limitless expansion of technological capability. A civilization that pursues only capability without understanding purpose may possess the power to change everything, yet not know where it should lead the world.

真正值得期待的，或许不是一个无所不能的文明，而是一个既拥有极高认知能力，又拥有足够深刻自我理解能力的文明。 What is truly worth hoping for may not be an omnipotent civilization, but one that possesses both extremely high cognitive ability and sufficiently deep self-understanding.

六、向前与向后，最终走向文明的另一端 VI. Forward and Backward, Toward the Other Side of Civilization

现在再回头看最初的两拨人，也许就能理解他们为什么正在走向两个看似相反的方向。 Looking back now at those two groups, perhaps we can understand why they are moving in two seemingly opposite directions.

向前走的人，试图通过技术突破智能的边界，创造新的存在形式，让文明拥有超越自身生物局限的能力。他们探索的是宇宙还能够孕育出什么。 Those walking forward seek to break through the boundaries of intelligence through technology, to create new forms of existence, and to give civilization the ability to transcend its biological limits. What they explore is what the universe might still be able to bring forth.

向后走的人，试图回到身体、意识、自然与生命的本源，理解那个一直以来被我们视为理所当然的存在。他们探索的是宇宙为什么能够产生意识，以及意识究竟意味着什么。 Those walking backward seek to return to the source of the body, consciousness, nature, and life—to understand that existence we have always taken for granted. What they explore is why the universe can give rise to consciousness, and what consciousness ultimately means.

但我们也不应该简单地将这两拨人划分为觉醒者和追随者、先进者和落后者。技术探索中有对未来的真正好奇，也有对错过时代的恐惧；精神回归中有真正的自我觉察，也有面对竞争失败之后的心理补偿。人的动机从来不是非黑即白的。一个人可以一边追逐技术浪潮，一边被 FOMO 驱动；也可以一边远离世俗竞争，一边真正获得精神上的自由。 But we should not simply divide these two groups into the awakened and the followers, the advanced and the backward. Technological exploration contains both genuine curiosity about the future and the fear of missing the times; spiritual return contains both true self-awareness and psychological compensation after competitive failure. Human motivation has never been black-and-white. A person can chase the tech wave while being driven by FOMO, and can also withdraw from worldly competition while genuinely attaining spiritual freedom.

真正重要的或许不是选择向前还是向后，而是能否看清自己为什么出发，以及是否拥有改变方向的能力。 What truly matters may not be whether one chooses to go forward or backward, but whether one can see clearly why one set out—and whether one has the ability to change direction.

我越来越觉得，人类文明的演化可能并不是一条简单的直线，而更像一个不断延伸的圆环。 I increasingly feel that the evolution of human civilization may not be a simple straight line, but more like an ever-extending circle.

我们从自然中诞生，在漫长的进化中获得意识；通过意识认识自然，通过科学改造自然，通过技术创造新的智能；而当智能发展到足够高的程度，我们又不得不重新面对意识、生命与存在的本源。 We are born from nature, and gain consciousness through long evolution; through consciousness we come to know nature, through science we transform nature, and through technology we create new intelligence. And when intelligence develops to a sufficiently high level, we find ourselves once again confronting the source of consciousness, life, and existence.

这是一条从自然出发，经过文明与技术，最终重新回到自然和存在本身的道路。 This is a path that sets out from nature, passes through civilization and technology, and ultimately returns to nature and existence itself.

或许，人类文明真正的终局，并不是无限制地积累财富、扩张疆域，甚至不只是创造一个比人类更聪明的超级智能。那些可能只是文明演化过程中的阶段性目标。更深层的目标，或许是让宇宙中的物质获得越来越丰富的认知能力，让智能不断突破自身的载体和边界，最终能够理解生命、意识、时间、空间乃至宇宙存在的规律。 Perhaps the true endgame of human civilization is not the limitless accumulation of wealth or the expansion of territory, nor even merely creating a super-intelligence smarter than humans. Those may be only interim goals along the path of civilization's evolution. The deeper goal may be to let the matter of the universe acquire ever richer cognitive ability, to let intelligence keep breaking through its own vessel and boundaries, and ultimately to understand the laws of life, consciousness, time, space, and even the existence of the universe itself.

当然，我们并不知道这条道路是否存在终点，也不知道所谓的终极认知是否真的可能实现。甚至，人类文明也可能在抵达这些问题之前，就因为自身的技术、资源或内部冲突而走向终结。文明并不必然进步，技术也不必然带来解放，宇宙更未必存在一个等待我们抵达的预设目的地。 Of course, we do not know whether this path has an endpoint, nor whether so-called ultimate cognition is truly attainable. Human civilization might even come to an end—through its own technology, resources, or internal conflict—before ever reaching these questions. Civilization does not necessarily progress, technology does not necessarily bring liberation, and the universe does not necessarily hold a preordained destination waiting for us to arrive.

但至少，我们可以选择文明的方向。 But at the very least, we can choose the direction of civilization.

如果说过去几千年，人类一直在追问如何更好地生存，那么未来几千年，我们或许会开始追问如何更深刻地存在。 If, over the past millennia, humanity has been asking how to survive better, then over the coming millennia, we may begin to ask how to exist more deeply.

向前，是创造尚未出现的智能；向后，是寻找已经存在的本源。 To go forward is to create intelligence that does not yet exist; to go backward is to seek the source that has always existed.

一个走向宇宙的未来，一个走向生命的起点。 One walks toward the future of the universe; the other walks toward the beginning of life.

而当这两条道路最终相遇，我们或许会发现：人类一直以来试图寻找的答案，既不完全存在于遥远的星空，也不完全存在于深邃的内心，而存在于宇宙、生命与意识之间那种尚未被我们真正理解的联系之中。 And when these two paths finally meet, we may discover that the answer humanity has always sought lies neither entirely in the distant stars nor entirely in the depths of the heart, but in the connection among universe, life, and consciousness—a connection we have not yet truly understood.

也许，人类文明并不是宇宙演化的终点，而是宇宙开始认识自己的一个起点。 Perhaps human civilization is not the endpoint of the universe's evolution, but a starting point from which the universe begins to know itself.

我们从星辰中诞生，创造智能，探索宇宙，最终试图理解星辰为何能够孕育出我们。 We are born from the stars, create intelligence, explore the universe, and ultimately try to understand why the stars could give birth to us.

而在这个漫长的过程中，我们真正需要警惕的，或许不是被机器取代，而是在拥有越来越强大的力量之后，却忘记了为什么出发。 And in this long journey, what we truly need to guard against is perhaps not being replaced by machines, but forgetting why we set out after gaining ever greater power.

这或许才是人类文明最漫长、也最值得期待的旅程。 Perhaps this is the longest, and most worth looking forward to, journey of human civilization.
